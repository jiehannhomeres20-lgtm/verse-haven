Privacy Policy

Verse Haven. Last updated: October 29, 2026.

Verse Haven is a small Bible reading website made as a school project. This page explains what information the site uses.

What we receive from Google

When you create an account, you verify it with your Google account. Google tells us your email address and name. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not ask for access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or any other Google data.

How we use it

We use your Google email only to check that you have a real Google account and to avoid creating two accounts for the same person. Your name and email are not used for advertising and are not sold or shared with anyone.

Where your information is kept

Your username, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, your email, and the verses you save are stored in your own web browser on your own device. They are not kept in a database on our servers. If you clear your browser data, they are removed.

Bible text and translation

Bible passages come from public Bible services. Some verses are sent to a free translation service when you choose a translated version, so they can be translated. Your name and email are not sent with them.

Your choices

You can delete your account information at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. You can also remove Verse Haven's access from your Google account settings.
